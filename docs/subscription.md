# Subscription & Billing

The **Subscription & Billing** page lets you manage your plan, enable jewelry configurators and buy user licences for your sales team. All charges are added to your monthly renewal.

---

## Subscription Screen List

| Tab | Description |
| --- | ----------- |
| **Overview** | Your current monthly total and what is included in your plan. |
| **Configurators** | Enable more jewelry configurators for your store. |
| **Users & Licences** | Buy user licence packs and assign them to your sales team. |
| **Billing History** | Every purchase and change to your subscription. |

---

## Pricing

| Item | Price |
| ---- | ----- |
| **Base platform fee** | $99.00 / month |
| **First configurator** | $49.00 / month |
| **Each additional configurator** | $5.00 less than the previous one |
| **User licence pack** (2 licences) | $15.00 / month |
| **First licence pack** | Included free with the plan |

:::note
A configurator keeps the price it was enabled at. The price shown on a new configurator is what it will cost if it is the next one you enable.
:::

## Configurators

Each configurator (Rings, Necklaces, Engagement Rings, Eternity Bands, Bracelets, Earrings, Pendants) is shown as a card.

| Card State | Description |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| **Active** | Already enabled. Shows the price it is locked in at and an **Enabled** label. |
| **Not enabled** | Shows the price if enabled next and a **Buy** button. |

### Steps to Enable a Configurator

1. Click **Buy** on the configurator card.
2. Review the configurator and its monthly price in the confirmation popup.
3. Choose a payment method and enter the payment details (see [Payment Details](#payment-details)).
4. Click **Pay** to enable it, or **Cancel** to close without changes.

The configurator is marked **Active** and its price is added to your monthly total.

## Users & Licences

### Buy User Licence Packs

Each pack gives 2 user licences.

1. Use the **-** and **+** buttons to choose the number of packs. The number of licences and the monthly price update as you change it.
2. Click **Buy**.
3. Review the order, choose a payment method and click **Pay**.

After purchase, the new licences and their 4-digit PINs are shown on screen. Share these PINs with your sales team.

### Your User Licences

Every licence you hold is listed as a card, showing whether it is assigned to a user.

| Action | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Bind** | Assign an unassigned licence to a user. Select the user and click **Save**. |
| **Rebind** | Move an assigned licence to a different user. |
| **Unbind** | Remove the user from the licence so it can be reused. |
| **View Details** | Enter the licence's 4-digit PIN to see its details. |

Each user can hold only one licence, so users already assigned elsewhere are not offered in the list.

### Licence Details

After you enter the correct PIN on the keypad (or with your keyboard), the popup shows:

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| **PIN** | The licence PIN |
| **Issued** | Date the licence was created |
| **Assigned to** | Assigned user, or **Unassigned** |
| **Email** | Assigned user's email |
| **Role** | Assigned user's role |

Click **Done** to close. An incorrect PIN is rejected and you can try again.

## Billing History

| Item | Description |
| ---- | ----------- |
| **Monthly Renewal** | Your recurring monthly charge for the base plan, configurators and licence packs. Your subscription renews automatically every month. |
| **Info icon** | Opens the **Monthly Renewal Breakdown**, listing every item in your renewal with its date, whether it was included in the renewal or added later, and its monthly price. |
| **History entries** | Purchases and changes made after your plan started, each with a date and the amount added per month (`--` for changes with no charge). |

---

## Payment Details

The purchase popup shows the items being bought, the amount **Added to monthly total**, and a choice of payment method.

| Payment Method | Fields |
| -------------- | ------ |
| **Card** | Cardholder Name, Card Number, Expiry Date, CVV |
| **UPI** | UPI ID (a payment request is sent to your UPI app) |
| **Net Banking** | Select your bank from the list |
