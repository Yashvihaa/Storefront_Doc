# Price Display

The **Price Display** page controls whether customers see prices while building a custom design, and which price they see. It applies to the product configurator (Diamond Setup, Metal Setup and Congregator steps) and does not affect prices shown to you in the admin panel.

---

## Configurator Pricing

The **Configurator Pricing** card lists the available options, each with its own switch. Exactly one option is active at a time; the active one is highlighted and its switch is on.

| Option | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Price Display [TCCTECH price + Margins]** | Customers see TCCTECH's benchmark price plus your configured margins. |
| **Price Display [Own pricing + Margins]** | Customers see your own store pricing plus your configured margins. |
| **No Pricing Display** | Prices are hidden from customers throughout the configurator. Use this if quotes are handled separately, for example in-store or by a sales rep. |

Below the list, a short description of the active option is shown. The option names and descriptions are supplied by the system and may be worded slightly differently on your screen.

---

## Changing the Setting

1. Click an option, or turn on its switch.
2. The change is saved immediately and a "Saved -- *option name*" confirmation appears. There is no separate Save button.

While the change is being saved, the new option stays selected and the list ignores further clicks.

:::note
Clicking the option that is already active does nothing. To change the setting, select a different option. This page does not check **Add**, **Edit** or **Delete** permission separately.
:::
